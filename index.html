CRUNCH LAB • Q&A

Why are Lay's bags filled with air? +
It's actually 100% pure food-grade nitrogen! Nitrogen creates an oxygen-free cushion that prevents the chips from shattering during shipment while locking out humidity so every chip stays as fresh and crispy as the second it left the kettle.
How is the 63-decibel crunch calibrated? +
Potato moisture is continuously measured with laser spectroscopy down to 1.8% hydration. This micro-porous cellular structure fractures at high frequency upon bite, producing an acoustic soundwave calibrated for peak sensory gratification.
Where are the potatoes sourced? +
Every single potato is grown on generational family farms across 25 U.S. states. From field to kettle cooking, the journey takes less than 24 hours to guarantee maximum potato flavor.
How does the electrostatic seasoning work? +
Our seasoning spices are micro-charged with static ions as they enter the tumbling drum. Oppositely charged potato slices naturally attract the spice particles evenly across every curve, avoiding bare spots or heavy clumps.
